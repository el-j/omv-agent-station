Easy Installation

Get Started in 3 Steps

Step 1

Install OMV Plugin

Install the native Debian package directly on your OpenMediaVault server: 

sudo dpkg -i openmediavault-ai-orchestrator_1.0.0_all.deb
Step 2

Configure in WebGUI

Navigate to Services ➔ AI Orchestrator in your OMV WebGUI, paste your tokens, pick your chat service, and click Apply.

Step 3

Start Coding!

Message your bot from your phone: 

/task my-repo "Add unit tests and auto-push"
